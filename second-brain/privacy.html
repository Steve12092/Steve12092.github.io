Second Brain Recorder

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Second Brain Recorder is a personal Android app that records voice memos and saves them to the user's own phone and, optionally, the user's own Google Drive. The developer does not run any server and does not receive any of your data.

What the app accesses

  • Microphone. Audio is recorded only after you tap Record, and only until you tap Stop. A notification is shown the whole time a recording is running.
  • Storage on your phone. Recordings are saved in the Recordings/SecondBrain folder on your device.
  • Google Drive (optional). If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app uses the drive.file permission to create a SecondBrain/inbox folder and upload your recordings into it. This permission only allows access to files and folders the app itself created. The app cannot see or read any other file in your Drive.

How your data is used and shared

  • Recordings go only to your own phone and your own Google Drive.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Google account information is used only to upload to your Drive. The app stores which Google account you connected, on your phone only.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Second Brain Recorder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Drive is used only to provide the upload feature described above, is not transferred to anyone else, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Retention and deletion

  • Recordings stay on your phone and in your Drive until you delete them.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its settings. Recordings in Recordings/SecondBrain and in your Drive are kept until you delete them yourself.
  • You can remove the app's access to Google Drive at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
