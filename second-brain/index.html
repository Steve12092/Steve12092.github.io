Second Brain Recorder

A personal Android app for capturing voice memos: thoughts, events and ideas, recorded with one tap and kept as a permanent archive.

What it does

  • A home-screen widget starts and stops a voice recording with one tap.
  • Each recording is named by the date and time it started, for example 20261025T151023+0900.m4a.
  • Recordings are saved on the phone in Recordings/SecondBrain.
  • If you connect Google Drive, each recording is also uploaded to a SecondBrain/inbox folder in your own Drive.

Google Drive access

The app asks only for Google's narrowest Drive permission (drive.file). With it, the app can see and manage only the folders and files it creates itself. It cannot see, read or change anything else in your Drive.

Who it is for

Second Brain Recorder is a personal project, built for and used by its author. It is not distributed through an app store.
